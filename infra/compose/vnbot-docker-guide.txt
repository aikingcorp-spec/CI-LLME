VNBOT - GOI THU DOCKER

1. Cai va mo Docker Desktop (Linux containers / WSL 2).
   https://docs.docker.com/desktop/setup/install/windows-install/
   Chuan bi khoang 20 GB trong, cap cho Docker it nhat 6 GB RAM.

2. Giai nen TOAN BO file ZIP vao mot thu muc rieng tren may Windows.
   Bam CHAY-VNBOT.cmd. Khong can Git, Node.js hay pnpm tren may.

3. Lan dau nhap may chu SMTP, cong, tai khoan, mat khau ung dung va email
   gui. Gmail: smtp.gmail.com, cong 465, tai khoan Gmail va mat khau ung dung.
   Dung mail cua anh de nhan ma dang nhap that. Muon doi SMTP: CAU-HINH-MAIL.cmd.
   Mat khau chi luu trong file .env tren may anh, khong gui cho nguoi khac.

4. Doi Docker tai va build xong, trinh duyet tu mo:
   http://127.0.0.1:54173
   Nhap email, nhap ma nhan trong hop thu, vao VNBot.

5. Ket noi AI trong ung dung: Cai dat > Mo hinh, them ket noi va chon model
   anh co quyen dung. Key/uy quyen nhap trong app, goi tai ve khong co AI key.
   Tao bot, chon model vua ket noi, gui mot cau hoi de thu phan hoi that.
   Tiep tuc giao bot tao mot file, doc lai file, mo trang web va thu giong noi.
   Cho phep micro tren trinh duyet khi thu goi; goi voice can ket noi tuong ung.
   Neu model chay tren may Windows, dung host.docker.internal de container
   truy cap may host; khong dung localhost cua container.

DUNG-VNBOT.cmd dung stack va giu du lieu. CHAY-VNBOT.cmd mo lai.
KIEM-TRA.cmd hien trang thai. Neu loi build/start, giu cua so va gui dong loi.
Khong xoa Docker volumes hoac file .env neu muon giu bot, chat va key.
Stack nay dung project vnbot-ai-test, volume rieng va chi mo cong loopback 54173.
May tinh bot chay trong container; supervisor dung Docker socket cua Docker Desktop.

Day la goi source-build: lan dau Docker tu dung image tu ma nguon di kem,
khong phai image da build san. Can Internet de tai base image/goi phu thuoc
va de dung dich vu AI. Khong can tunnel de chat, dung cong cu va thu micro
tren chinh may nay. Remote/nhan webhook tu Internet chua nam trong bai thu nay.

Goi duoc kiem launcher va cau hinh Compose. Chua nghiem thu Docker Desktop
tren Windows hay AI that tren may anh. Khong coi day la ban phat hanh 0.4.0.
