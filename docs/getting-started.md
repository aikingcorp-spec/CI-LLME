# Getting started

VNBot gives you persistent AI bots. Each one has its own conversations, memory, routines, and
computer, and keeps working on the web, desktop, and mobile apps.

## Create your first bot

1. Sign in at [vnbot.run](https://vnbot.run).
2. Tell the bot its job in plain words. It asks for anything else it needs.

## Connect your apps

Bots work in the apps you already use: Gmail, Slack, Notion, GitHub, and
[1,400+ more](/integrations/). Ask a bot to connect one and approve the sign-in it sends you.

## Give it a schedule

Ask for a routine, such as "every weekday at 8am, summarize my inbox". The bot runs it on its own
computer and reports back in the same chat.
