# Install VNBot on your computer

Run VNBot on your own Mac, PC, or VPS. Your chats and data stay on your machine.

## Steps

1. Download VNBot from [vnbot.run](https://vnbot.run/#tai-ve).
2. Open the app and pick **This computer**. VNBot starts on your machine.
3. Create your account. The first account you create owns this install.
4. Connect a model: your own key, or a model server running on your machine.

## Before you start

VNBot runs its bot computers in Docker, so install Docker first. Leave the machine on if bots should
keep running routines.

## Always-on machine

For an always-on machine, such as a Mac mini or a VPS, install VNBot there the same way. From other
devices, choose **Existing instance** in the desktop app or **Use a custom server** in the mobile app
and enter its https address.

## Where your data lives

Chats, bot files, browser profiles, and the audit log stay on your computer. Content you send to a
model provider is processed by that provider under its terms. You choose the provider and the keys.
